# Support

## Community

The community edition is free to download and use.

- Report bugs or request features in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/WilliamGvyan/MacAppBox/issues).
- Ask usage questions and share setups in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/WilliamGvyan/MacAppBox/discussions).
- Download packaged builds from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/WilliamGvyan/MacAppBox/releases).

## Paid support

Paid support is intended for priority troubleshooting, migration assistance, customized builds, and feature development.

The payment channel is not active yet. It will be published after GitHub Sponsors or another supported payment provider is configured. Until then, open an issue with the `paid-support` label and describe the request; no payment should be sent through unverified links.

## Commercial edition

Future Pro features may be distributed as a separately licensed commercial edition. The community repository will remain available without a self-deleting trial or reinstall-blocking DRM.
